# Governance and Safety Boundary

## Prohibited outputs
- real-stock buy/sell recommendations
- target prices, stop-loss points, trade timing or guaranteed returns
- broker, fund-sales, exchange, wallet or bank API connections
- storing accounts, passwords, tokens, API keys, SMS codes or identity documents
- automatic subscription, redemption, transfer, rebalancing or order execution
- investment advice without appropriate licensing and human review

## Required controls
- investor purpose and suitability pre-check
- evidence ledger for market and product data
- product due diligence and conflict check
- AI Use Log and human verification
- stress testing and residual disclosure
- advisor/compliance review ticket before any formal advice

## Kill conditions
Stop or downgrade if:
- the user asks for a real trade recommendation
- data source is missing or conflicted
- investor purpose, time horizon, or drawdown tolerance is unclear
- risk level and product/category risk are mismatched
- AI outputs unsupported profit expectations
- the system is asked to connect to a broker or execute orders
- conflict of interest is not disclosed
