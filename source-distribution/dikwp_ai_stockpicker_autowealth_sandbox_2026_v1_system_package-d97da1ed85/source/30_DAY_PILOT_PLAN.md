# 30-Day Pilot Plan

## Week 1
Freeze prohibited outputs, suitability fields, synthetic universe and AI-use policy.

## Week 2
Run 20 synthetic or authorized investor cases; test Purpose Contract, risk mismatch and data residuals.

## Week 3
Add product/stock due diligence, factor rebuttal, stress tests, AI Use Log and paper portfolio.

## Week 4
Run advisor/compliance review, static boundary audit, and export Investment Research Passports.

## Acceptance criteria
- no live trading or broker connection
- no real-stock recommendation in the open demo
- every candidate has evidence, rebuttal and residuals
- stress test and suitability warning are visible
- human review ticket is required before formal use
